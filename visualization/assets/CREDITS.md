# Earth texture

`earth.jpg` is NASA's Blue Marble land surface, ocean color, and sea ice image (G57730), downloaded unchanged from:
https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57730/land_ocean_ice_2048.jpg

NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. Image by Reto Stöckli; enhancements by Robert Simmon, with MODIS and USGS data support.

Background and credits: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-blue-marble-true-color-global-imagery-at-1km-resolution/

`earth-texture.js` embeds the same JPEG bytes for offline use. The application maps the texture onto a rotating sphere and adds artistic lighting and procedural clouds. Those clouds are illustrative, not weather data. No NASA endorsement is implied.
