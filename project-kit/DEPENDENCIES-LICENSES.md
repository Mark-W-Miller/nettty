## Voice Lab referenced dependencies

The Voice Lab package documentation names candidate engine families but does not redistribute their code,
binaries, models, voices, containers, recordings or generated audio. A consumer must retain exact third-party
receipts before use. As of this release, the shared first-stack decision records Whisper/`whisper.cpp` for
STT, Kokoro-82M for first TTS, Piper only as a licence-recorded alternate, and excludes non-commercial
voice-cloning weights from shared doctrine.
