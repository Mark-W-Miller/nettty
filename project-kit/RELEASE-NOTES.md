text-to-speech and explicit recordings; no server, browser controller, model, voice, recording, deployment or
consumer adoption is supplied. Prior 0.1.7 behavior-executor material remains part of the Kit.

only the vendored Kit, preserving local supplements and recording delivery separately from runtime proof.

0.1.10 incorporates Lee’s voice-authoring lessons and the scoped consumer publication script.
